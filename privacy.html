Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Social Analytics Studio is a local desktop application. Its purpose is to display analytics from social-media accounts that you choose to connect.

Data we access

After you approve a platform's authorisation screen, the app may access the profile and analytics data allowed by that platform and its approved permissions.

Local storage

Analytics data is stored on your computer. Connection credentials are stored locally using Windows Credential Manager.

Sharing

The app does not sell your personal data. Data is only sent to the social-media platform when needed to authenticate or retrieve the information you requested.

Your choices

You can disconnect a connected account in the app. This removes its local authorisation credentials. You can also remove local analytics data from the app.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner.
